About the Farm

Outcast Acres Farm isn’t just a name, it’s a way of life. Built on hard work and a passion for cultivation, we started our 10-acre farm in 2014, balancing full-time hustles with caring for our dogs, horses, poultry, goats, and falcons. We believe in doing things differently, cutting through the noise to grow cannabis that stands apart. From seed to harvest, every step is handled with care to deliver premium cannabis for the New York market, no shortcuts, just quality.
